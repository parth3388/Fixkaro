Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the payment type, whether inspection or service work has begun, parts ordered, and the written terms supplied with the booking or plan.

Completed work

Charges for completed inspection, labour or approved work are generally not refundable. Eligible repeat issues should first be reviewed under the applicable workmanship-support terms.

Spare parts

Parts that have been installed, specially ordered or cannot be returned to the supplier may not be refundable.

Processing

Approved refunds are returned through the available payment method after verification. Bank or payment-provider processing time may vary.

Requesting review

Email support@fix-kar.in with your service reference, payment details and reason for the request.
