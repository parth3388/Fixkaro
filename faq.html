Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before you book.

How can I book a FIX-KAR service?

Submit the enquiry form, call +91 88242 76600, email support@fix-kar.in or contact us through WhatsApp.

Where is FIX-KAR currently available?

Our contact office is in Pali, Rajasthan. Service availability for your exact location is confirmed when you submit a request.

Are inspection and repair charges the same?

No. Inspection or visiting charges may apply separately. Repair labour and spare-part charges are explained after diagnosis.

Are spare parts included?

Spare parts are charged separately unless a written plan or quotation explicitly states otherwise.

When will the technician arrive?

The visit window depends on location, service type and technician availability. Our team confirms the expected schedule before the visit.

What happens if the same issue returns?

Contact support with your service details. Eligibility for a follow-up visit depends on the original work, warranty period and whether the repeated issue has the same cause.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, subject to our cancellation policy. Please inform us as early as possible so the service slot can be updated.

How do business service plans work?

We first understand your sites and equipment, then propose the service frequency, response process, coverage and commercial terms.
