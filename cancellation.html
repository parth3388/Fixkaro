Cancellation and Rescheduling

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Before technician assignment

You may request cancellation or rescheduling without a cancellation charge before a technician has been assigned or dispatched, unless otherwise communicated for a special booking.

After dispatch

A visiting or cancellation charge may apply if a technician has already travelled to the service address or if access is unavailable at the confirmed time.

Rescheduling

Rescheduled appointments remain subject to technician and location availability.

How to cancel

Call +91 88242 76600 or email support@fix-kar.in with your name, registered mobile number and service-request reference.
