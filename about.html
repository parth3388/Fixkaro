Who we are

One platform for the services your home and business need.

FIX-KAR is a convenient home and technology service platform designed to make appliance repair, maintenance and installation services simple, reliable and accessible.

Customers can request professional assistance for ACs, washing machines, refrigerators, televisions, geysers, water purifiers, kitchen appliances, computers, laptops, CCTV systems, networking equipment and other home-maintenance requirements.
